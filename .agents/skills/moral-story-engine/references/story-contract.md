# Story contract

KindPath compiles a parent's lesson into a child-safe adventure in five typed stages:

1. Moral policy converts the lesson into behavior, motive, consequences, repair, forbidden treatments, and one policy decision.
2. Premise generation produces exactly three story-first candidates. The moral controls the decision axis, not the external plot.
3. Story graph compilation produces a shared setup, one binary choice, two complete mini-arcs, branch end states, convergence bridges, and a shared finale.
4. An independent judge scores the graph without writing it.
5. Shot compilation converts the validated graph into eight bounded generation segments.

A premise passes the storyness contract only when it has a non-moral external goal, a meaningful relationship, escalation before the choice, setup/payoff, a constructive action requiring effort, a tempting alternative with an understandable motive, and a proportionate natural consequence.

The writer, editor, and judge are separate roles. A stage may retry after deterministic or semantic feedback, but invalid artifacts never flow downstream.

Parent approval occurs after the package passes validation and before any video quota is used.
